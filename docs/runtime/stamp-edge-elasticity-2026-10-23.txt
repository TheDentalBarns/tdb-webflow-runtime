Treatment stamp edge elasticity - 23 October 2026

Finite stamp strips previously clamped at each end and cancelled coast momentum.
They now resist pulls beyond the ends and return with a critically damped spring.
The native scroll position stays bounded; only the inner CMS track translates.
Outward resistance increases with stretch, capped below 80px (or 20% of the
viewport). Reversing follows the finger immediately instead of accumulating an
invisible over-drag. Throws hand their velocity at impact into a small spring.

Existing free-coast decay, selection, CMS links, confirmation behaviour and
opt-in continuous looping are retained. Re-grabbing holds the current pose;
selection smoothly releases the stretch while aligning to the chosen stamp.
Reduced motion clears stretch on release. Cancellation, vertical gestures and
disposal release edge motion. Authored inline translate is restored at rest.
The original scroll geometry is measured without the temporary translation.
No new CSS, DOM nodes, dependencies, requests or persistent idle frame loop.
